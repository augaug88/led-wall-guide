How a scene is switched

  • One Stream Deck button per scene.
  • Whoever is operating the Mac toggles the scenes.
  • If the scene buttons are not available, the Mac is likely not switched on.

Page 2 · LED SETTING. Tap a key to see what it switches.

Default brightness is 30%

Leave it there unless the room is unusually bright. Higher settings wash out faces on camera and shorten panel life.

Put it back when you're done

If you change brightness for a session, return to 30% before you leave so the next team starts from the standard.

Rules for anyone making slides

1

Build in 16:9 at 1920 × 1080

Standard widescreen. The wall takes care of placement.

2

Nothing important in the bottom 83 px

That strip falls off the wall. Footers and small captions down there vanish.

3

Arial, 65 pt minimum

Readable from the back of the room. Fewer words, bigger type.

4

Check it on the wall before the session

A quick look on the real surface catches cut-offs and small text.
